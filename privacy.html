Privacy Policy

The Non-Coder · Last updated 6 November 2026

The Non-Coder ("we") runs the Instagram account @noncoderhq, the YouTube channel @noncoderhq, and the website noncoder.unchurned.co. This policy explains what data our publishing tool ("NonCoder Publisher") uses.

What NonCoder Publisher does

NonCoder Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of The Non-Coder to upload videos and captions to our own Instagram and YouTube accounts and to read basic performance statistics (views, likes, followers) for those accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.

Data we access

  • Access tokens for our own Instagram and YouTube accounts, stored only on the owner's computer (macOS Keychain).
  • Aggregate statistics about our own posts.

We do not collect, sell, or share personal data of viewers or followers. Google user data obtained through YouTube APIs is used only to publish and measure our own content, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Website

noncoder.unchurned.co is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies or analytics. GitHub may log basic request data per its own privacy statement.

Purchases

Digital products are sold through Topmate, which handles payments and buyer data under its own privacy policy.

Removing access / deletion

You can revoke NonCoder Publisher's access at any time via your Google Account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or Instagram settings. To request deletion of any data, email noncoderhq@gmail.com.
